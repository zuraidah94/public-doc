# Rumah Kita

Rumah Kita is a Malaysian family life-sim that plays in a web browser. All in-game text is in Bahasa Melayu.

This folder contains the HF edition, art tools and design documents. Earlier editions are archived in the [Claude repository](https://github.com/zuraidah94/Claude/tree/65a55e3b895de1c43c751391361be4b9cfc68979/rumah-kita).

## Play online

- [Edisi Lukisan Tangan HF (versi rasmi)](https://zuraidah94.github.io/public-doc/rumah-kita/edisi-lukisan-tangan-hf/)

## How to play

Works on phone and computer. On a phone, "ketik" means tap; on a computer, click.

### Start a game

1. Open the play link above. The first load may take a few seconds.
2. Tap **Ketik untuk mula** on the title screen.
3. You start at **06:30, Day 1** with a family in **Taman Harmoni**.
4. **Tap a family member** to control them. Their name card appears under the clock.
5. **Tap furniture or the floor** to give an order: sleep, cook, bathe, or walk there. **Tap another person** to chat, joke or flirt.
6. **Tap the person you're controlling** to open their own menu: tidy the house, bake kuih, buy a car, go for umrah, adopt, 🎭 Drama and more. The tags on the right show each activity's cost.

### Everyday play

- **Keep needs up.** Eat, sleep, bathe, have fun and talk to people. A mood of 70+ makes skills grow 25% faster and helps at work.
- **Earn money.** Go to work in a good mood (62+) to raise performance. At 100, with enough skill, you get promoted. Skills such as cooking, logic and charisma grow through practice (kitchen, bookshelf, mirror).
- **Build and decorate.** In **Bina**, tap an item, then tap a spot to place it. Tap existing furniture to move it, or sell it back for 70%. Nice decor gives the room a mood bonus.
- **Grow the family.** Make friends, find a partner (JodohKu helps), marry, have children, and watch them grow up through school and university.
- **Explore.** Open 🗺️ Kampung to visit the town, the istana, the market and more. You can take over any household on the map.
- **Watch stories.** Open 🎭 Drama from the phone or the self menu. Your choices decide each story's ending 🏆. **Auto-main drama** plays scenes with automatic choices.
- **Let them live.** **Kehendak sendiri untuk semua** (free will), at the bottom of the Keperluan tab, makes everyone act on their own when you're not giving orders.

### Saving

- The game **saves automatically** in your browser whenever you switch tabs or close the page. There is no save button to press.
- Your save stays on **that browser on that device**. Another phone, computer, or browser starts fresh, and so does clearing browser data or a private/incognito window.
- **All three versions share one save slot** because they're on the same site. Playing one version continues, and overwrites, the save from another. Stick to one version per browser if you want to keep a family.
- **🔄 Mula semula** (at the bottom of the Keperluan tab, and 🔄 in the top bar) deletes the current family and starts a new game.

### Help and cheats

- **📘 Panduan**, at the bottom of the Keperluan tab, opens the in-game guide with pictures.
- **Cheats**, next to it, opens a box for cheat codes (money, full needs and more). Type `help` to list them, or see [the player guide](dokumen/panduan-rumah-kita.md#kod-tipu-cheats).
- Everything else is covered in [Panduan Rumah Kita](dokumen/panduan-rumah-kita.md).

### Edisi Lukisan Tangan HF: Sims-style controls

This copy keeps the whole game and adds a live mode in the spirit of The Sims 4 and inZOI. It keeps Rumah Kita's own Malaysian painted style: there's no plumbob, no Simlish and no copied interface.

**Camera**

| Do this | Computer | Phone / tablet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zoom in and out (1× to 3×) | Mouse wheel or trackpad pinch, **+ / −** keys, or the ➖ ➕ buttons | Pinch with two fingers, or ➖ ➕ |
| Look around when zoomed in | Drag with the mouse, or arrow keys / **W A S D** | Drag with one finger |
| Follow the selected person | **🎯** button or **F** | **🎯** button |
| Walls: Penuh / Potong / Rendah | **🧱** button or **C** | **🧱** button |

*Potong* (cutaway) lowers only the walls in front of the selected person, so you can see into their room.

**Build and buy** (tab **Bina**)

- The item you're placing shows see-through on the tile under the pointer. It's solid-looking where it fits, and faint where it doesn't.
- **↻ Pusing** (or **R**) turns the item before you place it. Kampung furniture turns all four ways; town furniture flips to face the other wall. Tap a placed piece and choose **↻ Pusing** to turn it later.
- Drag a placed piece with the mouse to move it. Tapping still opens Alih / Jual / Pusing.
- The search box and price buttons (≤ RM300, RM300–1,000, > RM1,000) filter the catalogue.

**Mood and actions**

- In the Keperluan tab, **Kenapa mood?** lists every reason the mood went up or down (room beauty, mess, hunger, sickness, traits, emotions) with the points and time left.
- The current action and the queue show as round icons on the top-left of the house. A ring fills as the action progresses. Tap ✕ to cancel one.
- A live card with the selected person's portrait, mood and top reasons sits on the house screen (top-right on phones). Tap it to open Keperluan.
- People who stand idle breathe and glance around instead of standing frozen.

**3D people (prototype)**

Nota: mod orang 3D lama sudah dibuang dari salinan ini; permainan guna orang 2D lukisan tangan.

**Art:** backdrops, cut-ins, house paintings, drama cards, family portraits and maps were sharpened with the open [Real-ESRGAN](https://github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN) model (`realesr-general-x4v3`). Painted portraits were cut out with [BiRefNet](https://huggingface.co/ZhengPeng7/BiRefNet) for the live card.

This copy shares its save with the other versions (same browser, same site). Furniture you turn here also shows turned in the other Edisi versions.

## Playing outside Claude

The game runs from a web server, including GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → deploy from the `main` branch). Opening `index.html` straight from disk may block some images, so a server is the safer choice.

A few features need Claude's artifact runtime and are switched off elsewhere:

- **Cloud save** (☁️ Save to cloud / Load from cloud). Saves still work in the browser's local storage.
- **Downloading generated images** (kad raya, salasilah poster, konvokesyen photo). The game asks you to take a screenshot instead.

`alat/pelukis/` loads Google Fonts from the internet.
